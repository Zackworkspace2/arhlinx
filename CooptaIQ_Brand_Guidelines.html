01 — Brand Story

The name is the
oldest idea
in enterprise.

In ancient Rome, cooptatio was the formal ceremony by which a new member was admitted into the Senate or a collegium. It was deliberate, structured, and permanent. Not casual. Not administrative. A real act of belonging.

CooptaIQ brings that ceremony into the modern enterprise — making the journey from new hire to confirmed employee as formal, verifiable, and human as it was always supposed to be.

What we believe

Onboarding is not an administrative task. It is the first act of a relationship. Done right, it produces people who know exactly who they are working for and why it matters.

Done wrong — or not done at all — it produces people who were never really admitted. Who drift. Who leave.

Classical

Rooted in two thousand years of formal admission and institutional credibility.

Intelligent

AI that does the work of building, testing and tracking — so HR focuses on people.

African

Built for the scale, context and ambition of the African enterprise market.

07 — Voice & Tone

Speak like a senior
professional. Not a startup.

CooptaIQ is used by CHROs and HR directors at large enterprises. The voice is authoritative without being cold, warm without being casual, precise without being heavy.

Precise
Say exactly what you mean. Every word earns its place. No filler, no hedging.
✓ "Your new hire passed 9 of 11 modules. Module 3 needs attention."
✗ "Great progress! Almost there!"
Credible
Speak from authority. CooptaIQ knows onboarding. It does not need to sell itself with enthusiasm.
✓ "Assessment completed. Record archived."
✗ "Awesome! You crushed it!"
Human
Formal is not cold. Every communication acknowledges that there is a real person on both sides.
✓ "Welcome to [Company]. Your onboarding begins here."
✗ "User onboarding sequence initiated."
Direct
State the action. State the outcome. Do not make the reader infer what happens next.
✓ "Sign off on this hire to confirm their employment."
✗ "Please review when you have a moment."

09 — Name Usage Rules

CooptaIQ is one word.
Always.

The name has specific rules. Breaking them erodes brand consistency across every piece of communication.

CooptaIQ
✓ Correct
Coopta IQ
✗ Do not split into two words
COOPTAIQ
✗ Do not use all caps
cooptaiq
✗ Do not use all lowercase
Coopta-IQ
✗ Do not hyphenate
CoopatIQ
✗ Do not misspell
Do
Write CooptaIQ as one word with capital C and capital IQ
Use the full name on first mention in any document
Pair the wordmark with the corridor motif in visual contexts
Use Fraunces Bold to set the wordmark in type at display sizes
Keep the IQ suffix in Brass in all approved colour contexts
Use the name as a proper noun — "use CooptaIQ" not "use the CooptaIQ"
Do not
Split the name across two lines between Coopta and IQ
Place the logo on backgrounds that compromise legibility
Alter logo colour outside the four approved background contexts
Stretch, skew or rotate the wordmark in any direction
Use the logo at sizes smaller than 120px wide in digital contexts
Abbreviate the name to "Coopta" or "CIQ" in public-facing material
